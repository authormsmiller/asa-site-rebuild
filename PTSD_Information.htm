Medical Research & Health Evidence

Clinical studies, research documentation, and medical evidence correlating combat trauma with metabolic and cardiovascular conditions in military veterans.

The Association of Post Traumatic Stress Disorder and Metabolic Syndrome: A Study of Increased Health Risks in Veterans

Pia S. Heppner, MD et al. • Published in Boston Medical Journal (BMC) • January 9, 2009

This clinical study, conducted in direct cooperation with the Veterans Administration, demonstrates a statistically significant relationship between combat-related Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) and baseline diagnostic markers of coronary heart disease.

The research identifies measurable physiological indicators elevated in veterans diagnosed with combat trauma:

Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure Waist-to-Hip Ratio HDL Cholesterol Serum Triglycerides Plasma Glucose Concentration

Relevance to VA Secondary Service-Connection Claims

This medical publication can serve as supporting medical documentation for veterans and surviving families establishing service-connection or secondary disability claims with the Department of Veterans Affairs regarding heart disease linked to combat-related PTSD.
