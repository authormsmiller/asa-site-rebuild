U.S. Military Replacement Medals & Awards

Instructions, National Archives procedures, and required standard forms for requesting replacement military decorations at no expense.

If you were never issued your military decorations, or if they were lost or destroyed in a fire, storm, flood, or hurricane, you may request official replacements from the U.S. government at no personal expense.

Expected Timeline: Initial government confirmation typically takes around 2 months, followed by 5 to 6 additional months for medal processing and delivery. Please be prepared for an extended review window.

Applicants must complete Standard Form SF-180 and supply supporting military personnel documentation (along with any citations or orders in your possession) to expedite verification.

1 Review Official National Archives Guidelines

Visit the National Archives site for current instructions regarding replacement medals, awards, and military records:

National Archives: Replacement Medals & Awards Portal
2 Complete and Mail Standard Form SF-180

Print and fill out Standard Form SF-180. Mail your completed form, custom cover letter, and supporting documents to the Military Personnel Records Center in St. Louis:

National Personnel Records Center
Medals Section (NRPMA-M)
9700 Page Avenue
St. Louis, MO 63132-5100

Foreign Awards Notice: Federal replacement requests apply solely to decorations authorized and issued directly by the U.S. Armed Forces. Foreign decorations—such as the Republic of Vietnam Campaign Medal—are not distributed by the NPRC and must be acquired through authorized military surplus distributors or specialty ribbon providers.
