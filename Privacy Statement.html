Privacy Statement

Information gathering, member record-keeping, and communication policies for the Angry Skipper Association, Inc. website.

Welcome to the official website of the Angry Skipper Association, Inc. (“ASA”), a tax-exempt non-profit 501(c)(19) organization consisting of veterans who served in Delta Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry Regiment (Airborne), 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile) between 1965 and 1972.

This is a private association website maintained for our members and extended unit family. We have adopted the following policies to demonstrate our dedication to member privacy and disclose our information gathering and dissemination practices.

Information Collection and Use

ASA is the sole owner of the information collected on this website. We reserve the right to share specific contact details with trusted third-party service providers contracted directly to deliver association services for ASA and its membership.

Information collected from ASA members is utilized strictly to fulfill direct requests, including ordering ASA materials, submitting reunion registrations, or requesting veteran assistance. ASA will not sell, share, or rent this information to any external party in ways differing from what is stated in this policy.

Email Communications

The email addresses of ASA members and newsletter subscribers are strictly protected and will never be sold or rented to commercial third parties, business partners, advertisers, or outside benefit providers. We share member email addresses solely with our contracted email distribution vendor, who is legally bound to refrain from using them for any other purpose or releasing them to outside entities.

Members who update or submit their contact information are subscribed to the association newsletter. Users may opt out of future mailings at any time by utilizing the unsubscribe instructions included at the bottom of each email dispatch, or by contacting the ASA Secretary directly to be permanently removed from email rosters.

Use of Cookies

Cookies are standard text tokens that websites transfer to a device for record-keeping and session management.

Website Cookie Policy: The Angry Skipper Association website is not set up to plant tracking cookies on your computer or monitor external web activity. While standard web browsers allow users to adjust settings to notify or block incoming cookies, browsing the ASA site places no advertising or tracking beacons on your machine.
